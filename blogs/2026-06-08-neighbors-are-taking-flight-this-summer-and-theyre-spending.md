Summer is almost here, and for the people on Nextdoor, it's already well underway in their heads. According to new research from Nextdoor's Customer Insights &amp; Analytics team, 91% of neighbors are planning at least one leisure trip this summer — 14% more likely than the general population. More striking: they're planning to spend $7,400 on average, which is 87% more than the typical American consumer.
